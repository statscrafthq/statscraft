# Sample data

`survey.sav` is an invented customer survey of a fictional home internet provider, with 300 respondents. Every value is drawn at random by `make-survey.sps`, so no real person is in it, and running that file again gives the same data. Each respondent has a number (`id`), a `region` (North, South, East, West), `gender`, `age` in years and an `age_group`, the highest `education`, and a survey `weight` around 1. They rated their overall `satisfaction` from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied), with 9 for "Don't know", which is a missing value. Three yes or no questions ask whether they would `recommend` the provider, whether they `contacted` customer support in the last year and whether they are thinking about `switching` to another provider. Five variables `ch_web`, `ch_app`, `ch_phone`, `ch_store` and `ch_email` mark the channels they used to reach the provider, and the multiple response set `$channels` groups them. `spend` is the monthly bill in euros (empty for those who would not say), and `interview_date` is the day of the interview. Every variable and every code has a label.
